- You must have a pre-recorded or live demonstration.

## Judging criteria (verbatim)

Judges listed: DDoSKi & his not-so judgy buddies · Cal Hacks 13.0

- **Application:** Projects should have a feasible real-world use. Could someone realistically adopt this, and would it deliver a clear benefit? Strong submissions identify who they help, what problem they solve, and why people would actually use them.
- **Intentionality:** Projects should offer an intuitive, engaging experience for their target users, guided by clear first principles. Every design choice should feel purposeful, with a clear sense of who it's for and why it works the way it does.
- **Functionality:** Projects should work as intended, with a live demo that runs smoothly free of major bugs. Submissions should also include a clear, meaningful video demo on devpost for sponsors evaluating asynchronously.
- **Creativity:** Projects should show original thinking and a fresh perspective, with inventive problem framing and unexpected combinations of ideas or technologies. Clearly show what makes your project stand out from similar work.
- **Technical Complexity:** Teams should show a command of the frameworks and infrastructure behind their project, with sound architectural choices and engineering depth that goes beyond basic implementation.
