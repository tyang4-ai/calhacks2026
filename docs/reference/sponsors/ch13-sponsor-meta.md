## What Cindia v2 does for this track

The brief's line: "Buyers connected to the actual maker and their community, storefronts, community projects."

- **The maker, not a middleman:** every piece page shows who made it, their story, the hours of work and "Where your money goes". The receipt later says "Paid to the maker".
- **Community pages:** each community says who it is in its own words (demo text, labeled), its craft, how its makers are verified and what it doesn't sell.
- **Community projects:** anyone can fund something like an apprentice stipend or a weaving school, with a contribution at checkout. Works in progress are commissions paid in stages as the maker reaches each milestone.
- **Storefronts:** each community or studio has its own page at its own link.
- **AI that brings people closer:** vibe search turns a feeling into pieces from real makers. The support swarm answers "Did the maker stitch this herself?" from the community's own records, and says "I'll ask the maker" when it can't.
