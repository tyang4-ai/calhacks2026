# Annapurna Labs · co-host, networking

Not one of my tracks. Annapurna Labs (an Amazon company) has no prize track this year, since the Hacker Guide dropped their prize section. They are worth meeting for internships and new grad roles in chips and the software stack around them. Hub: [[cal-hacks-13]]

## At a glance

- **Who:** designs the custom silicon behind AWS (Trainium), plus the firmware and software on top
- **Booth:** Sponsor Booth 12. Saturday 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- **Workshop:** Sat 3:00 to 4:00 PM in the Theater, engineer-led. The Hacker Guide calls it a "Technical Domain Talk." The email says it's for expert, intermediate and beginner hackers.
- **Networking:** Sat 6:00 to 8:00 PM at Gate 6, for casual 1-on-1 chats with their engineers. Their Oct 20 email said 4:00 to 6:00 PM, but Hive and their Slack post now say 6:00 to 8:00.
- **Hiring:** interns and new grads across all software and hardware teams
- **Sign-ups:** the "Annapurna Labs x CalHacks 13.0 Talent Community," plus a separate Amazon talent community for general Amazon, AWS and Amazon Robotics roles. Both links are in their Slack posts in #cohost-annapurna-labs.
- **Sponsor tier:** Anchor (co-host)
