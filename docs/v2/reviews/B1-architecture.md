# B1 · Architecture and harness review

Reviewer: code and system design, through three lenses (staff frontend engineer, build-harness engineer, test engineer). Oct 24, 2:50 PM PT.

Read: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/harness/v2-runbook.md`, `docs/v2/README.md`, `docs/v2/contracts.md` (all, section 11 last), `docs/common/events.md`, `docs/common/ui-contract.md`, `docs/plans/README.md`, and the plans I2-core, I2-kit, I2-shell, I2-art, I2-tour, I3-redesign, S3-catalog, F-search, F-process, F-support, F-communities, F-seller-hub, F-storefront, F-watch, M-buyer, M-approvals, M-maker-statement and M-desktop. Code: `src/core/`, `src/sources/`, `src/app/`, `src/ui/index.ts`, `src/main.tsx`, `tests/demo/`, `tests/smoke/`, `tests/data/`, `scripts/gate.mjs`, `scripts/test-feature.mjs`, `playwright.config.ts`, the edit guard (`.claude/hooks/lib/policy.mjs`), the gate and cut skills, and the agent files. I also checked the B12 and B14 adapter plans where they meet the registry.

Could not run: any `npm` command, because `node_modules` isn't installed in this checkout. Every finding below comes from reading plans, contracts and code, with file and line references.

**Verdict: not ready.** Fix the six blockers and it becomes ready with fixes.

The event-store core is sound. Reducers stay idempotent through `put`, `addId` and `same`. `seek()` resets and replays the timeline, and a person's pending answer drops itself when the epoch changes. Every plan keeps features isolated: reads go through `useCindia`, writes through `userEvent` and `emit`, and I found no feature-to-feature import. The trouble is that the v2 plan set was written in two passes and never reconciled with section 11, so several handoffs point at files nobody creates, or at shapes two plans define differently. Six problems stop a gate as the plans are written. I2-core's type widening breaks five feature files and the v1 catalog, which no V1 agent may edit, so V1 step 1, G1b and G2b can't pass. `src/core/nav.ts` has no owner. I2-core and I2-shell map the Phase A cameras differently. The gate times the whole Playwright run, which runs on one worker on a 2 vCPU VM and goes over both the 75-second and 130-second limits, and G5 and G6 never learn the tour's length. The shell and F-process both render the process test IDs. Section 11's extra request rows have no task. Beyond those there are 22 majors at plan boundaries: shapes, IDs, props the shell drops, data that breaks figure 11, and gates that see other agents' half-written files. The test plan also leans on 2 to 4 second wall-clock windows and fixed-time seeks that will flake on the VM or break after I2-tour. Most fixes are one-line plan edits or one small extra task.

## What holds up

- The store, the player and the registry already support v2's pattern: one reducer per type, idempotent per entity ID, replay on seek, and answers to people that drop themselves when the epoch changes.
- Section 11's ID scheme (`*_user_<n>`, counted from the store) is deterministic and starts over after a reset.
- The live adapters are planned around the registry as it really works. B14 caches one live call per run for the per-event `produce()` calls. B12 seeds in the background under the 8-second health timeout.
- The test hooks, the slot error boundaries and the placeholder IDs carry over unchanged, so a cut feature still leaves a labeled slot.
