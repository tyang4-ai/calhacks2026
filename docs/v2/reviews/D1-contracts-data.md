# D1 · Contracts and data consistency review

**Verdict: inconsistent.** 8 blockers, 19 majors, 27 minors.

## Summary

I read the v1 contracts (`events.md`, `data-model.md`, `ui-contract.md`, `demo-script.md`, `glossary.md`), all of `docs/v2/contracts.md`, the 24 v2 plans, and the current `src/mock/index.ts`, `src/core/types.ts`, `src/core/state.ts`, `src/core/events.ts`, `src/core/validate.ts`, `src/core/store.ts`, `src/sources/requests.ts`, `src/sources/registry.ts`, `src/mock/events.ts`, `tests/demo/beats.ts`, `tests/data/` and the v1 live adapters. For every shared name I traced the producer, any step that changes it, and every consumer.

Much of it holds. Every slot has exactly one feature. Every test ID in section 8's On screen column is rendered by some plan. The keys on the tour's keyed events match `EVENT_KEYS`, with one missing override. Figures 12, 14 and 15 are produced by data and checked by tests.

The problems sit in four places.

1. **Section 11 came after the producer plans.** It settled many names, but I2-core, I2-shell, I2-kit, I2-tour and S3-catalog still describe the earlier version in places. So eight feature plans import names no task builds (`src/core/nav.ts`, the kit `Link`, the `log` store key), and five scripted request answers have no owner.
2. **Two sides build different shapes.** Producer and consumer disagree on `ScriptedScan`, `Policy`, `ScriptedRun.final`, the `approval` object on `approval.requested`, storefront block props and listing facets.
3. **Three gates fail as written.** G1b fails on the Phase A camera mapping and the `process-` test IDs. G2b fails on the figure 11 chips. G4v2 fails on the d01 and d02 pages and on the v1 timeline tests.
4. **The person-scoped ID scheme collides.** It breaks when two answers are pending at once, when a scan repeats, and against SpacetimeDB rows that outlive a reset.

**Severity**

- **Blocker:** followed as written, the plans fail G1b, G2b or G4v2, or a P0 feature depends on a name or a producer that no plan task creates.
- **Major:** followed as written, they fail a feature's or an upgrade's own gate (G3 or G5), build two incompatible shapes across a boundary, or contradict a section 11 decision. The V1 gates and the tour still pass.
- **Minor:** a naming, ownership or wording gap a builder can settle inside its own scope, or an edge case no gate checks.

Locations are `file:line`. "contracts" means `docs/v2/contracts.md`. Plan files go by their ID, so "I2-core:61" is `docs/plans/I2-core.md` line 61.
